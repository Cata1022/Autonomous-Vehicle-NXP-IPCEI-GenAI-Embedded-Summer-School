<<<PROJECT_IDEA>>>
Autonomous Car - self-driving vehicle that uses computer vision and TinyML on a
microcontroller to follow a road, recognize traffic signs and traffic lights,
and navigate autonomously in a controlled environment

Vision & AI Processing Unit: ESP32-S3 CAM board equipped with an OV3660 camera module.
This board handles all the computer vision tasks, neural network inference,
and logic decisions due to its hardware vector acceleration and PSRAM.

Hardware Execution Unit: NXP FRDM-MCXA153 microcontroller. This acts as the physical
execution layer. It receives high-level commands and uses bare-metal programming
to generate precise PWM signals for motor control and steering.

Sensors: The OV3660 camera is the primary sensor. Additional ultrasonic or IR distance
sensors will be connected directly to the NXP board for immediate, low-latency
obstacle detection.

Chassis: A motorized rover platform equipped with drive motors (DC with H-Bridge).

Lane Keeping (Classic Computer Vision + PID):
Runs on Core 0 of the ESP32-S3 at a high frame rate (20-30 FPS).
The camera captures the road, applies a Region of Interest (ROI) mask, and uses image
thresholding (binarization) to isolate the lane lines.
The algorithm calculates the center of mass (centroid) between the lines to find the
desired setpoint.
A PID controller calculates the steering error and sends the steering angle and speed data
to the NXP MCXA153 via a UART connection.

Traffic Sign Recognition (Edge AI / TinyML):
Runs asynchronously on Core 1 of the ESP32-S3 at a lower frame rate (3-5 FPS).
Uses a trained TinyML Object Detection model to classify complex semantic elements like
STOP signs or directional arrows.
When a sign is detected with high confidence, this core sends an override command
(e.g., "Brake for 3 seconds") to the NXP board, temporarily bypassing the PID lane-keeping
data.

Obstacle Avoidance (Reactive Logic):
Handled directly by the NXP MCXA153. If the physical distance sensors detect an immediate
blockage, the NXP triggers a hardware-level emergency stop, ignoring all UART
movement commands from the ESP32-S3 until the path is clear.
